How a piece is made

Three things happen
between the market and the door

01

Picked that morning

Fruit is chosen off The Fresh Market floor the day it is used — which is why what is in season shows up in the arrangements before it shows up in the catalogue.

02

Carved and set by hand

Melons are scored and shaped, berries threaded, roses set one at a time into the frame. Nothing is assembled in advance, so sizes and colours can be changed on request.

03

Delivered the same day

Pieces are boxed upright and sent out across the UAE the same day they are built, because none of this keeps.

Talk to the store

Before you order

Questions

How quickly can one arrive?

Pieces are hand-made in Dubai and delivered the same day across the UAE. Because every arrangement is built to order rather than taken off a shelf, the day's cut-off and delivery window are confirmed on the store at checkout.

Can a piece be customised?

Yes — several of the pieces here started as commissions and stayed in the catalogue, including the Customized Pokémon Fruit Platter and the two Eid Customize Arrangements. Colours, fruit and size can be specified; order the customised pieces directly from the collection and add your brief.

What does a piece cost?

From AED 45 for a small dates and nuts box up to AED 9,500 for a Royal Gourmet showpiece. Half the collection sits under AED 400, and the most common range is AED 250 – 600.

What should I send to a large gathering?

For a majlis, a wedding or an office, the Royal Gourmet pieces and the large fruit hampers are built to be set down as a centrepiece and eaten from. For one person, a fruit bouquet or a ready-to-eat platter travels better.

Is everything on this page live?

Every piece, price and photograph here is pulled from the Ambrosia collection on the store. Prices are in UAE dirhams, as published on 9 November 2026 — the store itself is always the current source.
